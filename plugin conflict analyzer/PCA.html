Record types by volume
Type reference
WRLD / CELL — worldspace & cell data. Very common; best resolved with a merged patch.
COBJ — crafting recipes. Losses can break workshop menu entries.
LVLI — leveled lists. High gameplay impact — affects loot spawns.
ALCH — consumables/chems. Losses can change chem effects or food stats.
QUST — quest records. Can affect dialogue, objectives, flags.
PERK — perks. Low volume but high impact if lost.
OMOD — weapon mods (misc mods). Losing these breaks mod crafting/attach.
NAVM — navmesh. Losing these can break NPC pathing.
NPC_ — NPC records. Stats, factions, AI packages.
WEAP — weapons. Losing these breaks weapon stats or keywords.
ARMO — armor/apparel. Affects item stats and crafting components.
