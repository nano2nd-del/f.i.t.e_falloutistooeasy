I made some xEdit scripts to make patching this a little bit easier!

It just batch replaces the keywords for BNAM.

Also has the crafting bench keyword for Simple Standalone Universal Crafting Bench.

It's pretty easy to use, you have to edit a line to point to your patch

    Create your ESP in xEdit
    Add simple crafting's keyword to the ESP
    Apply Filter for COBJ
    Add the modded COBJ's to your ESP
    Highlight the entries in your ESP
    apply the script for the bench you want them to go to
